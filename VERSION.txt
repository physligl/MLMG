MLMG v0.10.3
Release date: 2026-09-21
Public source release based on the current canonical manuscript state.
Reorganizes the first-part solver narrative so Chapter 3 becomes a general linear-system foundation covering direct methods, stationary iteration, Krylov subspaces, CG, GMRES, preconditioning, and FGMRES.
Moves multigrid-specific mesh-dependent convergence and smoothing analysis into Chapter 4, so two-grid correction follows directly from the failure mode of local stationary solvers.
Revises the Chapter 3 mental model: M^{-1} is treated as an operator application rather than an explicit inverse, Krylov methods are presented as subspace correction methods, CG as A-energy minimization with A-conjugate directions, GMRES as residual minimization realized through Arnoldi, and FGMRES as a variable-preconditioned search over stored correction vectors.
Normalizes terminology so established Chinese technical terms use Chinese while Krylov, Arnoldi, matrix-free, and standard algorithm acronyms remain in English where that improves recognition.
